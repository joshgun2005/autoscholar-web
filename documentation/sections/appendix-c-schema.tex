\chapter{Database Schema Reference}
\label{app:schema}

This appendix provides the complete database schema for \autoscholar, covering all 20+ tables with column definitions, types, constraints, and relationships.
The schema is defined in Drizzle ORM format with both SQLite (\file{shared/schema-sqlite.ts}) and PostgreSQL (\file{shared/schema.ts}) variants.

\section{Schema Differences: SQLite vs PostgreSQL}
\label{sec:schema:diff}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Key differences between SQLite and PostgreSQL schemas.}
\label{tab:schema-diff}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{lXX}
\toprule
\textbf{Feature} & \textbf{SQLite} & \textbf{PostgreSQL} \\
\midrule
Timestamps & \texttt{integer} (Unix epoch, mode: 'timestamp') & \texttt{timestamp} (native) \\
Booleans & \texttt{integer} (mode: 'boolean') & \texttt{boolean} (native) \\
Vectors & \texttt{text} (JSON string) & \texttt{vector(1536)} (pgvector) \\
Serial IDs & \texttt{integer} (auto-increment) & \texttt{serial} \\
Text types & \texttt{text} (all sizes) & \texttt{varchar(N)} / \texttt{text} \\
API Keys table & Included (\texttt{apiKeys}) & Not in schema.ts (added at runtime) \\
Write concurrency & Single-writer (WAL mode) & Full MVCC \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}
